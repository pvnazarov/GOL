GOL Haiku Summaries

How they were made

Claude Haiku through the claude CLI, four at a time. Each book gets about 34,000 characters: the whole text when short, otherwise excerpts from its start, middle and end. Title and author come from the catalogue. EROTIC and SEX-TECH folders were not sent.

Spot checks

34 random summaries read across both runs: no serious errors, minor slips in about one in five (a wrong relationship, a doubtful date). Lesser-known books are summarized from the excerpts alone; known books also draw on Haiku's own knowledge. Three books whose text is mostly Latin letters were redone in Russian.
